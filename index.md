I am an Intermediate Research Fellow at the [Clinical Trial Service Unit and Epidemiological Studies Unit](https://www.ctsu.ox.ac.uk/) at Oxford Population Health. I work with the [Mexico City Prospective Study](https://www.ctsu.ox.ac.uk/research/prospective-blood-based-study-of-150-000-individuals-in-mexico), leading research-capacity building in Mexico in collaboration with Mexico-based researchers.

## Research

My work brings together large-scale prospective studies, genetic epidemiology, and biomarker research to understand how disease develops and affects health. I am particularly interested in cardiovascular, kidney, and metabolic disease, and in generating evidence that is relevant to populations often under-represented in biomedical research.
